About

Tarkov Loot Checker is a free tool that helps you decide in seconds whether to keep, sell or stash the loot you find in Escape from Tarkov.

What it does

  • Search items by English or Japanese name, short name or common nickname, and see the flea price, take-home after fees, best trader price and value per slot.
  • See how many of each item you need for quests, Kappa and the hideout, and whether they must be Found in Raid.
  • Track finished quests, hideout levels and items you have, and the site works out how many more you need.
  • Separate prices for PvP, PvE and seasonal wipes.

Data

Price, quest and hideout data comes from the community-run tarkov.dev and is refreshed every 5 minutes. Thank you to everyone at tarkov.dev.

Flea fees are estimates based on the widely known formula. Game updates can change how fees work, so check the in-game listing screen before big trades.

How the site is run

  • This is an unofficial fan site run by an individual and is not affiliated with Battlestate Games.
  • Game names and images belong to Battlestate Games and their respective owners.
  • To cover running costs the site may show ads or accept donations. Ads never change the data shown.
  • Your progress is stored only in your own browser and is never sent anywhere.
