Keep, sell or stash? How to decide

Backpack space is limited. Run through these four checks in order and the decision gets easy.

1. Will you need it?

If a quest or hideout upgrade needs the item, selling it means buying it back later, and Found in Raid items cannot be bought back at all. Kappa (The Collector) items are the most painful to lose. Items for quests you have already finished do not count, which is why tracking your progress matters.

2. Value per slot

For everything else, compare value per inventory slot, not the price. A 2-slot item worth 300,000₽ is 150,000₽ per slot; a 1-slot item worth 200,000₽ is 200,000₽ per slot and the better pick. When your bag is full, swap out the lowest value-per-slot items first.

3. Is it Found in Raid?

Many quest hand-ins require Found in Raid (FIR) items, which you only get by picking them up in a raid and extracting alive. An FIR item that a quest needs is worth more to you than its price.

4. Where does it sell best?

The flea often pays more but charges a listing fee that grows quickly when you list above the item's base price, and each item has a minimum player level for listing. The site compares the flea take-home after fees with the best trader price and shows whichever pays more.
