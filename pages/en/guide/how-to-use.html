How to use Tarkov Loot Checker

1. Pick your mode

Choose PvP, PvE or Season at the top. Flea prices differ by mode, so prices and verdicts follow the mode you pick. The site remembers your choice.

2. Search

Type an item's name, short name (e.g. GPU, LEDX) or Japanese name. On desktop press / to jump to search and Esc to clear it. With an empty search the site lists the items worth the most per inventory slot.

3. Read the verdict

LabelMeaning
Keep for KappaNeeded for quests that lead to the Kappa container.
Keep for questsNeeded for quests. Keep it until you have enough.
Keep for hideoutNeeded for hideout upgrades.
You have enoughThe items you recorded cover what you still need.
No longer neededYou have finished every quest and upgrade that uses it.
Safe to sellNot used by any quest or hideout upgrade.

"FIR only" means the hand-in requires the item Found in Raid; bought copies will not count. The big number on the right is the take-home after fees divided by the slots the item takes.

4. Track progress

Open Progress to tick finished quests and set each hideout station's current level. With "Also complete prerequisite quests" on, ticking the quest you are on marks every earlier quest in its chain as done. In search results, open Details and use + / − under "You have" to record how many you own.

5. Move to another device

Progress is stored only in your browser. In Progress → Transfer, copy your progress code and paste it on the other device, then press Load.
